# Changelog

Each version's list becomes its release notes and is shown in the update prompt. Add "(critical)"
after a version number to have installed copies update without asking.

## 1.6.1
- The Trebuchet now points out of the picture, and one very large penguin pops up to squeegee the glass, wiping the snow off stroke by stroke
- The Christmas Card prints a real photo of the crew as they stood when the flash went
- Carol singers sing with eyes open and beaks wide
- Santa's hat sits on his head; his chair is centred on its rug

## 1.6.0
- Eight new scenes: The Trebuchet (they find the glass), The Christmas Card, Santa's Grotto, The Countdown to Christmas, Santa's Workshop, Christmas Biscuits, Carols round the Tree, and Decorating the Town
- The Big Ramp is rebuilt: no more waiting or walking over the mound, and the big ramp gets used. Off the kicker, out of the top of the picture, and straight down through the snow
- Belly-slides redrawn: the body lies flat and the head rides up, looking where it's going
- Celebrations are rarer and varied: a proper cheer for the big moments, a nod or a few claps for the small ones, and no two penguins cheer the same way
- Penguins no longer idle with their face behind someone, half off the edge of the screen, or across the join between screens
- The full-screen preview shows scene, seed and time in the corner, so a screenshot can be replayed exactly
- New scene: The Long Table. The crew sets up a hall after Leonardo's painting, each one brings something to share, and for a moment the table becomes the painting
- New scene: Nativity Play. The crew builds the stable, then puts on the play: shepherds and a wandering sheep, an angel on a hay bale, and three kings
- Giant Snowman: the two rollers push the ball side by side, both with their flippers on the snow
- Belly-slides: the penguin lies in the snow with its flippers held back, ploughing a little wave of snow, and only one slides at a time
- Snow drifts in from past the edges of the screen, so the bank builds up evenly instead of in a hump
- Preview timeline: scrubbing holds the picture while it catches up, follows the drag, and the arrow keys work after dragging

## 1.5.1
- New scene: Hockey Night. The rink gets resurfaced, the goals are pushed in, and every game ends differently
- New scene: Gingerbread Bake-Off. The crew builds a TV set, then three very different bakers compete for the trophy
- Giant Snowman: you can see Pip get rolled up in the snowball, feet sticking out
- Tug-of-War: the rope no longer flickers out on one screen, and nobody stands on the join between screens
- Deck the Tree: everyone queues at the decorations box instead of crowding into each other
- Penguins step when they move nearer or further away, instead of sliding
- Rocco's sunglasses are now mirrored lenses in pale frames, so they don't look like red eyes
- Blizzard: snow builds up in lumps on hats and backs instead of a smooth cap
- Belly-slides: flippers dig in turn, snow sprays, and the penguin rocks along with each stroke

## 1.4.0
- One crew at a time across your screens, on the screen you choose in Settings
- Updates now ask first, with what's new: update, remind me later, or skip
- Full-screen preview has a timeline: drag back to any moment (arrows jump 5 s, comma/full stop step a frame)
- Snow melts away smoothly when you come back, even mid-blizzard
- The blizzard keeps moving through the white-out instead of looking frozen
- Penguins belly-slide across long distances, squash when they land, and step round each other
- Flying Lessons: they dig the hill, carry in the board and seesaw, and Rocco's flip lands clean
- Igloo Contest: the judges carry their desk in, scores are random, and blocks sit on heads
- Candy Parade: dancers queue before the bar goes up; canes stay clear of faces
- Rocco's sunglasses have red frames
- Fixed a "File not found" page that could appear after an update
