# Changelog

Each version's list becomes its release notes and is shown in the update prompt. Add "(critical)"
after a version number to have installed copies update without asking.

## 1.5.0
- New scene: Hockey Night. The rink gets resurfaced, the goals are pushed in, and every game ends differently
- New scene: Gingerbread Bake-Off. The crew builds a TV set, then three very different bakers compete for the trophy
- Penguins walk when they move nearer or further away, instead of sliding

## 1.4.0
- One crew at a time across your screens, on the screen you choose in Settings
- Updates now ask first, with what's new: update, remind me later, or skip
- Full-screen preview has a timeline: drag back to any moment (arrows jump 5 s, comma/full stop step a frame)
- Snow melts away smoothly when you come back, even mid-blizzard
- The blizzard keeps moving through the white-out instead of looking frozen
- Penguins belly-slide across long distances, squash when they land, and step round each other
- Flying Lessons: they dig the hill, carry in the board and seesaw, and Rocco's flip lands clean
- Igloo Contest: the judges carry their desk in, scores are random, and blocks sit on heads
- Candy Parade: dancers queue before the bar goes up; canes stay clear of faces
- Rocco's sunglasses have red frames
- Fixed a "File not found" page that could appear after an update
